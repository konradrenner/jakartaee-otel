== Let's talk about O11y

[%step]
* The capability to understand the internal state of a system by analyzing the data it produces
* Combines signals to give a holistic, correlated view of system behavior over time
* By correlating signals, O11y surfaces patterns and anomalies that static monitoring might miss
* *So:* monitoring tells you when something is wrong; observability helps you understand why

* Lets have a look at the apps, as you see, you don't see direct connection between the services in the logs, let's change this
* now that we already have tracing in place and made the logs trace aware, lets look in to the power of traces
* image you want to see important infos direct in the trace => span attribute & span event
* image you have some process which is not directly connected to the trace, but some kind of "linked" to it (e.g. something which started based on an event)
* but context propagation can provide you even more, not just technical details of a trace, but also propgation of application-defined properties
* I think now is the right time, to also have a look at metrics
* many technical metrics are already in place thanks to instrumentation, but maybe you want to create some custom metrics
* In our case we will use the contextual information out of the baggage and build a metric on top of it (be aware and careful of dimensions of metric time series)
* one word to technical functionality => otlp is normally a push mechanism. so an o11y backend has not to scrape all of the time (like with prometheus), it just gets the data
